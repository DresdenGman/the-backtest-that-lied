What This Changed

  • A clean negative result is worth more than a profitable-looking lie.
  • Statistical significance ≠ economic investability.
  • The hardest skill is killing your own ideas when the evidence says no.
  • Best system design: build something that can prove you wrong.

"I began trying to predict stock returns.
I finished learning how easily a researcher can predict the past."
