\section{Data and Methodology}

\subsection{Data}
The research used the RQAlpha A-share daily market-data bundle, covering 5,511 historically listed stocks. A point-in-time (PIT) universe was constructed without survivorship filters, resulting in 4,073 stocks appearing across the full sample period. Daily OHLCV data was aggregated to monthly frequency.

\textbf{Important:} True point-in-time fundamental data (market capitalization, book value, earnings, industry classification) was unavailable. All analysis was conducted using price and volume data only. This limitation is fundamental to the project's conclusion.

\subsection{Feature Set}
Twenty ranked technical indicators were computed at monthly frequency:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
    \item \textbf{Momentum:} 5-, 10-, 20-, 60-, and 120-day returns (M5, M10, M20, M60, M120)
    \item \textbf{Volatility:} 5-, 20-, and 60-day standard deviation of returns (S5, S20, S60)
    \item \textbf{Volume:} 5- and 20-day average turnover ratios (V5, V20)
    \item \textbf{Moving Average:} 20- and 60-day MA deviation (MA20, MA60)
    \item \textbf{Channel Position:} Position within 20-day high-low range (POS)
    \item \textbf{RSI:} 14-day relative strength index (RSI)
    \item \textbf{Turnover:} Current and 5-day average turnover (TN, TN5)
    \item \textbf{Reversal:} 1- and 5-day reversal (REV1, REV5)
    \item \textbf{Cross-sectional:} 20-day cross-sectional rank (X20)
    \item \textbf{Skewness:} 20-day return skewness (SK20)
    \item \textbf{Breadth:} Number of advancing stocks over 20 days (N20)
\end{itemize}

All features underwent cross-sectional rank normalization to $[-1, 1]$ within each month. This normalization was recomputed separately for each validation month during training to prevent information leakage.

\subsection{Inference}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
    \item \textbf{Information Coefficient (IC):} Cross-sectional Spearman rank correlation between predicted and realized returns, computed monthly.
    \item \textbf{ICIR:} Mean IC divided by standard deviation of IC.
    \item \textbf{Newey--West:} Heteroskedasticity- and autocorrelation-consistent standard errors with 3 and 6 lags.
    \item \textbf{Block Bootstrap:} 1,000 resamples with block length of 6 months, 95\% confidence intervals.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Null Infrastructure}
Three adversarial tests were applied to every specification:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*),leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{iid Gaussian null:} Replace target returns with independent standard normal draws. Expected: IC $\approx 0$.
    \item \textbf{Constant-label test:} Assign identical labels to all observations. Expected: standard deviation of predictions $\approx 0$.
    \item \textbf{Permutation audit:} Shuffle labels across stocks within each month, retrain, check correlation with original predictions. Expected: $\approx 0$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Portfolio Construction}
Monthly rebalancing with 90/80 no-trade bands:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
    \item At month-end: rank stocks by ensemble prediction.
    \item Select top 10\% by rank percentile.
    \item Enter at $\geq 90$th percentile; retain at $\geq 80$th percentile.
    \item Execute at next trading-day open.
    \item Equal weight within portfolio.
\end{itemize}
