\section{The Three Collapses}
\subsection{Collapse I: Statistical Illusion}
Initial belief: 20 factors + LightGBM could predict returns with IC 0.940.
Detection: Date-shift scan peaked at $k=0$, not $k=+1$.
Correction: IC collapsed 0.940 $\to$ 0.100.
Interpretation: 89\% of apparent predictive power was mechanical illusion (absolute decline: 0.84 IC points).

\subsection{Collapse II: Economic Implementation}
Initial belief: IC 0.108 should produce positive portfolio returns.
Results: U50 executable (31bp costs): net -0.9\%, DD +61.3\%. Benchmark: -5.9\%.
Interpretation: Statistical predictability $\neq$ economic investability.

\subsection{Collapse III: Scalability}
Initial belief: Full universe profitable (U100 net +6.8\%).
Finding: +48.1\% of holdings in Q1. Within-Q1: +2.7\% monthly excess.
Interpretation: The alpha existed where capacity was severely constrained.
